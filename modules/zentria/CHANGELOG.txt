##  v1.3.1 (2025-06)
- Integration of a detection sensor for the presence of a two-factor authentication (2FA) module on your site

- Added monitoring sensors for database volumetry
- Preparation for data collection for the upcoming system page, including:
    - history of modified files
    - system information from the AdminInformationController
    - list of overrides
    - web services informations
- Updates to business sensors to collect data needed for auto-threshold calibration during the first user test
- Adjusted SEO sensors to ignore disabled languages
